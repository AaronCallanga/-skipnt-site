Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Skipnt is a planner you use by chatting: it turns what you type into tasks, notes and calendar events. This policy explains what data Skipnt handles, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. Skipnt is made by Aaron Dave D. Callanga ("we"). Questions: aaroncallanga.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your tasks, notes, events and chats are stored on your phone.
  • When you chat with the assistant, your message and the context it needs are sent to Google's Gemini AI to work out what to create or answer.
  • We keep a small account record (your Google sign-in, trial and plan status, and how many AI requests you made this month).
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Menu → Delete account).

AI features

The assistant can make mistakes. Every change it makes shows up in the chat and can be undone. Don't send it passwords, health information or other sensitive details you wouldn't want processed by an AI service.

How long we keep data

Account, plan and usage records are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete these records and your backups straight away. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for tax and accounting law. Data on your phone stays until you delete it, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Menu → Delete account. This also clears the app's data on that phone. You can also request deletion without the app.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll reply within 30 days.
  • Stop using AI: simply don't use the chat assistant; everything else works without it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act or the EU GDPR) you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each account read its own records. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

Children

Skipnt is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
