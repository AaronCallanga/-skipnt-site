Delete your Skipnt account

Skipnt, by Aaron Dave D. Callanga

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Skipnt and tap the menu icon (top right).
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email aaroncallanga.dev@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Skipnt account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

DeletedKept
Your account and sign-in link, trial and plan status, AI usage counts, and any backups. In-app deletion also clears the app's tasks, notes, events and chats on that phone. Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, as tax and accounting law requires. Data on a phone where you only uninstall the app without deleting stays there until you uninstall.

Subscribed? Deleting your account doesn't stop billing. Cancel in the Google Play Store: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Skipnt.
